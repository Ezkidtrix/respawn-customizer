# Respawn Customizer
A simple geode mod that allows you to change and customize the respawn time per-level, meaning for one level you could respawn in 1s, and another could be 0.5s.

# How to use
Simply install the mod, open a level and open the pause menu, click the button labeled "RS" on the left side bar, and then customize the settings there (the time format is in milliseconds, or seconds × 1000). When you close the menu or press escape, the settings should save and you'll be good from there! :)
